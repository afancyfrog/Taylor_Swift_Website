About This Site

This website aims to categorize songs in a new way by finding recurring images and themes in an artistic discography.

There is an artistic discovery process behind this website, allowing users to explore recurring motifs and related lyrics, song titles and physical releases.

This is not a complete archive. It is a proof of concept and will still be developed further.
